Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026 (app version 1.4.0)

DiploMentions: Her Diplomacy is a learning app with quizzes, role-play dialogues, Maya's Story and a Toolkit for women at work, online and in business. The lessons work offline. Everything in the app is free; there are no purchases. The app uses the internet only for ads and Google's consent message. This policy explains how the app handles information in version 1.4.0.

Information We Collect

We (DiploMentions) do not collect personal data. The app has no login or account, and it does not ask for your email address, phone number, contacts, camera, microphone or location. The only personal detail it asks for is an optional name for your certificates, which stays on your phone. Everything you type or save in the app is kept on your phone (and in your phone's own backup, if you use it).

One Google service built into the app processes some data under Google's own policies: Google AdMob, which shows the ads that keep the app free. It is described below. Google Play's in-app review window, if it appears, is run by Google Play.

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • The app is free for everyone and shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner at the bottom of some list screens, now and then a full-screen ad at a natural break (after you finish a dialogue, or when you leave the end of a Maya chapter), and ads you choose to watch for a small optional extra ("Watch an ad to …": remove one wrong answer, or double today's Daily Challenge Sparks). An ad never unlocks lessons or stories; everything is open to everyone.
  • Ads are never shown in or right after safety lessons, safety dialogues or safety chapters (including their content notes and end screens), or in Private Notes, Reset, the privacy, terms, licence and disclaimer pages, the welcome screens, or a screen where you are typing. Full-screen ads never appear in your first minutes after installing, at most one every four minutes and six a day.
  • AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID and app set ID, your IP address (to estimate an approximate location), information about your device, how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information. Google processes this for advertising, measuring ads, preventing fraud and analytics, as described in Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
  • Consent: when the app opens, Google's consent tool checks whether your region needs your choice (for example the UK, the EEA and some US states). Where it does, the consent message asks before any ad is requested, and no ad is loaded until your choice is known. If you choose not to allow personalised ads, Google can show non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time with "Privacy choices" in Settings, where your region needs it.
  • On newer Android versions, the ads SDK may also use Android's Privacy Sandbox (Topics and attribution reporting) where your phone offers it; you can manage this in your phone's privacy settings.
  • Your consent choice is stored on your phone by Google's consent tool; the app itself does not read or store it.
  • For Google Play's Data safety form, data that the ads SDK collects counts as collected by the app, so the store listing says the app collects and shares the data above. We (DiploMentions) never receive it.
  • Ad requests are marked as not directed at children and for a general audience (maximum content rating G), and sensitive categories are blocked.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (for example Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).

Your Documents and Private Notes

Your brand kit, business details (name, area, how to pay, PDF label language), quotes and invoices, payment reminders, money and committee records, interview answers, raise plan, certificate name and Private Notes are stored on your phone. Private Notes are encrypted with a key made from your PIN (the app keeps only a check value and the number of wrong tries, never the PIN itself); we cannot read them, and if you forget the PIN nobody can recover them. Private Notes are excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. While Private Notes are open, screenshots and the recent-apps preview are blocked. When you share notes as a PDF, the app deletes its temporary copy afterwards. If Android backup is on, your phone may include your progress and Toolkit documents (never Private Notes) in its own backup to your Google account; we cannot see it. Images and PDFs (posts, invoices, notes, certificates) are made on your phone and go only where you choose to share them.

Reminders

The optional daily reminder is off by default. The app offers it once, after you have opened the app twice; you choose the time, and you can turn it off with one tap in Progress. It, and any payment reminders you set, are local notifications scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us or anyone else. Reminder text never mentions a safety topic. The daily reminder's text is neutral: "Daily Challenge: 5 quick questions are ready." For reminders the app uses these Android permissions: show notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, asked for only when needed, on Android 13 and later), run after the phone restarts (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) and vibrate (VIBRATE). It does not use exact alarms.

Tip of the Day Widget

If you add the optional "Tip of the day" widget to your home screen, it shows one short work, business or money tip a day from a list inside the app. It never shows safety topics, your progress or anything you have typed, reads and stores nothing, and uses no internet. Tapping it opens the app.

Read Aloud

Read aloud is optional and only starts when you tap it; a Stop button is shown while it reads. It uses a text-to-speech voice that is already installed on your phone and works offline: the app sends the text only to the speech engine on your phone (provided by your phone's maker or Google), never over the internet, and never uses a voice that needs the internet. Read aloud stops when you leave the screen or the app. If no offline voice is installed, the app shows how to add one in your phone's settings. Private Notes are never read aloud.

Sharing and Rating

Share buttons open your phone's own share options with a picture, file or message made on your phone (for example your Sparks, a Brand Studio post, a message, a quote or invoice, Maya's ending card or a certificate). A short line with the app's Google Play link can be added to what you share. In Settings, "Add app link when sharing" has two choices: "Share cards and Share the app" (on unless you turn it off) and "Messages and documents" (Message Builder, Raise Planner, Interview Kit, quotes, invoices and reminders; off unless you turn it on); the link carries a simple label (for example utm_source=share, utm_medium=message) so Google Play can count installs by source. It contains nothing about you. The line is never added to Private Notes, safety lessons or anything you copy. "Share the app" in Settings opens the same share options with a short invitation; the app never reads your contacts and never sends anything by itself. The app does not upload anything; it goes only where you choose to send it. Now and then, after you finish a dialogue or a Maya chapter (never after safety content or an ad), the app may show Google Play's in-app review window; Settings also has a "Rate this app" link. Reviews are handled by Google Play.

Local Storage

The app stores your progress on your phone using Hive local storage: quiz and dialogue progress, XP, streaks, the days you used the app (for your streak and weekly goal) and your weekly goal, Sparks (including Daily Challenge Sparks), your chosen focus, light or dark appearance, Maya's Story reduce-motion choice, the Message Builder tone, language and "Writing to" choice you used last, whether the welcome screens and the read-aloud tip were shown, your last finished activity (for Continue), badges, Maya's Story progress, endings, the cards you collected, the story panels you have seen and your notebook notes, certificate dates, the day's Daily Challenge, your reminder setting, when the review window was last offered, the disclaimer and terms versions you accepted, whether to add the app link when sharing, how many times the app has been opened, when it was first opened, and when the last full-screen ad was shown and how many today (so ads are spaced out). Reset progress on the Progress screen deletes your progress, but keeps your settings (appearance, focus, weekly goal, Maya's Story reduce motion), the disclaimer and terms you accepted and the ad spacing. Your progress and Private Notes stay on your phone: we do not send them anywhere, and Private Notes are encrypted. Your Toolkit documents and Private Notes are kept until you delete them or uninstall the app.

Deleting Your Data and Your Choices

Reset progress deletes your progress. Each Toolkit tool has its own delete, and Private Notes has Delete all notes. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone. You can change your ad consent with Privacy choices in Settings (where your region needs it), and reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. We hold no data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete. Google keeps ad data under its own policies.

Internet Connectivity

Lessons, dialogues, Maya's Story and the Toolkit work without an internet connection. Ads and Google's consent message need a connection.

Android Permissions

  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): for ads and the consent message.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) and, on newer Android versions, Android's ad services permissions: used by Google AdMob.
  • Wake lock and background work (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for its own background tasks.
  • Notifications, restart and vibrate: for the reminders above.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions.

Website and Glossary Links

The Visit DiploMentions button and some dialogue glossary links open diplomentions.com in your browser. The app sends nothing to that website, which has its own privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob, with Google's User Messaging Platform for consent (ads; no other ad networks or mediation)
  • Google Play in-app review (the review window, run by Google Play)
  • No purchases or billing, no Firebase, no Google Analytics, no Crashlytics, no sign-in, no cloud sync and no user accounts

Children's Privacy

Her Diplomacy is for adults (18 and over). It is not directed at children, and ads in the app are not targeted at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. If you have a concern about a child's use of the app, contact diplomentions@gmail.com.
