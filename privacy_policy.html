Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 9, 2026

Brand Spark Quiz is an offline-first quiz app for learning branding, marketing, psychology, storytelling, and market research basics. This policy explains how the app handles information in the current version.

Information We Collect

Brand Spark Quiz does not collect personal data. The app does not require a login, user account, email address, phone number, name, location, contacts, camera access, microphone access, or any other personal information.

The current version does not include ads, Firebase, Google Analytics, Crashlytics, Sign in with Google, cloud sync, push notifications, or background services.

Local Storage

Brand Spark Quiz stores quiz progress locally on your device using Hive local storage. This may include quiz progress details such as completed quiz count, XP, streaks, best score, badges, and last quiz date.

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us, uploaded to a server, shared with third parties, or synced across devices.

Internet Connectivity

Brand Spark Quiz is designed to work offline. The current release version does not require an internet connection for quiz content, progress tracking, or normal app use.

Third-Party Services

The current version of Brand Spark Quiz does not use third-party services for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, authentication, cloud storage, or personal data processing.

  • No Google AdMob
  • No Firebase
  • No Google Analytics
  • No Crashlytics
  • No Sign in with Google
  • No cloud sync
  • No user accounts

Children's Privacy

Brand Spark Quiz does not knowingly collect personal data from children or any other users. Because the app does not collect personal information, there is no personal data from children for us to store, sell, share, or process.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app changes. If future versions add features such as ads, analytics, accounts, Firebase, cloud sync, or other services, this policy and the app's store disclosures should be updated before that version is released.
