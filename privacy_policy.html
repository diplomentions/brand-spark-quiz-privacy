Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 30, 2026

DiploMentions: Her Diplomacy is an offline learning app with quizzes and role-play dialogues for women at work and women entrepreneurs. This policy explains how the app handles information in the current version (1.2.0).

Information We Collect

Her Diplomacy does not collect personal data. The app does not require a login, user account, email address, phone number, name, location, contacts, camera access, microphone access, or any other personal information.

The current version does not include ads, Firebase, Google Analytics, Crashlytics, Sign in with Google, cloud sync, or push notifications sent from a server.

Optional Daily Reminder

The app has an optional daily reminder for the Daily Challenge. It is off by default. If you turn it on (after a Daily Challenge or on the Progress screen), the app asks for permission to show notifications and then shows one local notification a day, at a time you choose, with the neutral text "Daily challenge: 5 quick questions are ready." The reminder is scheduled on your phone only; no information is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn it off at any time on the Progress screen, and Reset progress turns it off too.

For this reminder the app uses these Android permissions: show notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, asked for only when you turn the reminder on, on Android 13 and later), run after the phone restarts (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, so a reminder you turned on keeps working after a restart), and vibrate (VIBRATE, for the notification). It does not use exact alarms.

Sharing and Rating

On some results and on the Sparks screen, a Share button opens your phone's own share options with a short text and a picture of your Sparks. The picture is made on your phone and never names a topic. The app does not upload it; it goes only where you choose to send it.

The app may occasionally show Google Play's in-app review window, and the About screen has a "Rate this app" link to the Play listing. Reviews are handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy; the app itself does not collect or send any information about them. The app only remembers on your phone when it last offered the review window, so it does not ask too often.

Local Storage

Her Diplomacy stores quiz progress locally on your device using Hive local storage. This may include quiz progress details such as completed quiz count, XP, streaks, best score, badges, Sparks, finished topics and dialogues, dialogue scores and last quiz date, plus the day's Daily Challenge questions, your reminder setting (on or off, and the time), when the review window was last offered, and which version of the disclaimer you accepted. Reset progress on the Progress screen deletes it.

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us, uploaded to a server, shared with third parties, or synced across devices.

Internet Connectivity

Her Diplomacy is designed to work offline. The current release version does not require an internet connection for quiz content, progress tracking, or normal app use.

Website and Glossary Links

The Visit DiploMentions button opens diplomentions.com in your browser, and some dialogues link to a glossary page on that site. The app does not send anything to that website. It has its own privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

The current version of Her Diplomacy does not use third-party services for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, authentication, cloud storage, or personal data processing.

  • No Google AdMob
  • No Firebase
  • No Google Analytics
  • No Crashlytics
  • No Sign in with Google
  • No cloud sync
  • No user accounts

Children's Privacy

Her Diplomacy does not knowingly collect personal data from children or any other users. Because the app does not collect personal information, there is no personal data from children for us to store, sell, share, or process.
